## Generated examples

The examples are AI-generated interpretations, not photographs of physical paintings, prints, stitches, or mosaics.
They were generated with Google Nano Banana 2 in Paper.
The outputs were separated from their backgrounds and mapped to the site's palette.

Paper generation file:
https://app.paper.design/file/01M34C2EBAEY49KBTYMVA8GH3T

Verify access and node IDs before reusing the Paper file. The library does not depend on that file remaining available.

Theme pigments:

- Forest: `#355744`.
- Brown: `#855b3a`.
- Sage: `#5a7561`.
- Graphite: `#343b32`.

The original page background is `#f5f0e2`. The individual example files retain transparency instead of this color.

## Reuse limits

- The prompts are reusable style recipes, not a claim of exact reproduction of the original generations.
- Use a new source photograph when the subject changes.
- Do not use a Queenstown example as evidence of another location's geography.
- Contour study lines are artistic marks, not survey data or navigational information.
- Retain source and generation notes when delivering these examples outside this library.
- Keep the separate photo-editorial and rubber-stamp poster skills unchanged.
