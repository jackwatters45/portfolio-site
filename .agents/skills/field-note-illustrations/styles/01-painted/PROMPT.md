# 01 · Painted

Opaque paint shapes with dry bristle marks and a few pencil accents.
This is softer than Carved and more descriptive than Dry brush.

Read the [shared prompt](../../references/prompts.md). Add the actual scene and palette, then this style block.

## Style prompt

```text
Paint a small observational gouache or acrylic illustration from the reference.
Use a few flat, opaque shapes with slightly irregular edges.
Show dry bristle tracks, broken paint edges, and small gaps between marks.
Keep the shapes handmade, not clean filled polygons.

Add only two or three light pencil accents. Do not outline every form.
Let blank space describe the light areas, including snow, sky, and open water when present.
Use no more than four muted pigments.

Keep the main forms recognizable without copying every detail.
Remove unnecessary ridge lines, buildings, leaves, and other fine marks.
The result should feel quiet and carefully observed, not like a detailed panorama or watercolor wash.
```

## Preparation and checks

- Start with the default cutoff, `0.065`. Retain partly transparent bristle edges.
- Map the primary, accent, and softer pigments separately when present.
- Keep broad painted forms and sparse pencil accents readable at page size.
- If the result looks like a filled vector, request broken brush edges rather than added texture over everything.
