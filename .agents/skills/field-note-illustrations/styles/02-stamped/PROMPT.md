# 02 · Stamped

A sparse, worn rubber-stamp impression using two spot inks.
This is lighter and more open than Carved.

Read the [shared prompt](../../references/prompts.md). Add the actual scene and palette, then this style block.

## Style prompt

```text
Make a small, sparse hand-carved rubber stamp impression.
Use one primary ink and one small secondary spot-ink accent.
Retain the distinctive contours and a few essential forms.

Leave large empty spaces inside the scene.
For a landscape, keep water and snow mostly empty.
Use only a few short, broken water lines when needed.
Avoid individual trees, windows, and dense buildings.

Make the ink look printed with uneven pressure.
Use ragged gouged edges, broken contours, large dry-ink gaps, pinholes, and occasional slight double impressions.
Let the small secondary ink area sit slightly out of register.

Keep an open, irregular silhouette.
Do not add a rectangle, seal, circle, postage perforations, official stamp border, or logo.
```

## Preparation and checks

- Use two source/target ink pairs. Start with cutoff `0.065`.
- Keep worn gaps transparent. Do not fill them to increase contrast.
- Check that small pinholes remain visible at page size.
- If the image is too heavy, reduce solid ink areas rather than lowering the whole image's opacity.
