# 03 · Pencil

Broken colored-pencil contours with loose, selective hatching.
The grain and pressure changes distinguish this from Etched and Contour study.

Read the [shared prompt](../../references/prompts.md). Add the actual scene and palette, then this style block.

## Style prompt

```text
Make an observational colored-pencil field sketch.
Use one muted primary pencil and one very small earthy accent.
Draw grainy, broken contours with changing pressure, a few doubled lines, and sparse loose hatching.

Shade only three or four small areas.
Keep most of the scene as empty space.
Use confident, imperfect marks instead of continuous technical outlines.
Let secondary details fade or remain unfinished.

Preserve the important contours, directions, and relationships in the source.
Suggest buildings with a few small roof marks only when they help recognition.
Do not make a detailed architectural drawing.

Keep the pencil strong enough to read at small website sizes.
Avoid gray background haze, paper grain, and dense shading across the whole scene.
```

## Preparation and checks

- Use two pigment pairs. Try cutoff `0.045` only if `0.065` removes delicate marks.
- Check that a lower cutoff does not retain a faint background rectangle.
- Preserve grain within pencil strokes, not a texture field behind them.
- If lines disappear at page size, request stronger pressure rather than uniform thick outlines.
