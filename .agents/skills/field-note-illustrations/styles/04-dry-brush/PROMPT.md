# 04 · Dry brush

Broad, scratchy paint gestures with large empty gaps.
This is more abstract than Painted and wider in stroke than Brush pen.

Read the [shared prompt](../../references/prompts.md). Add the actual scene and palette, then this style block.

## Style prompt

```text
Reduce the reference scene to seven or eight broad, confident flat-brush marks.
Use two or three muted pigments.
Keep the main silhouette and spatial relationships recognizable, while removing most small details.

Show scratchy bristle tracks, broken paint edges, uneven pressure, and quick changes in direction.
Let substantial empty gaps separate the strokes.
Use opaque gouache on a nearly dry brush, not a translucent wash.

Use broad sweeps for important forms.
Do not add thin outlines, fine pen detail, or many separate objects.
Do not fill empty sky merely to create a background.

The result must remain an open arrangement of paint marks, not a painted rectangle.
Let each broad stroke do useful work in describing the scene.
```

## Preparation and checks

- Start with cutoff `0.065`. Include softer pigment pairs only when present.
- Retain transparent channels within the bristle strokes.
- Keep the strokes broad enough to differ from Pencil and Brush pen.
- If the subject becomes unclear, restore one essential contour rather than adding many small marks.
