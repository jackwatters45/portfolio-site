# 05 · Carved

Strong ink masses with wide cut-out channels.
This is heavier than Stamped and less detailed than Woodgrain.

Read the [shared prompt](../../references/prompts.md). Add the actual scene and palette, then this style block.

## Style prompt

```text
Create a bold hand-carved print using a few strong ink masses.
Use one dark primary ink and one small, slightly misregistered accent.
Keep the source's essential proportions and relationships.

Cut open channels through the solid forms with the appearance of a wide V-gouge.
Simplify distinctive peaks, roofs, coastlines, or other forms into a few recognizable cuts.
Use rough gouge notches, grainy ink, uneven pressure, and substantial dry-ink breaks.

Give the outer silhouette irregular printed edges.
Leave open gaps between important forms.
Keep the illustration bold without turning it into a generic logo.

Avoid dense hatching, individual leaves, detailed windows, and continuous fine outlines.
Do not add geometric badges, enclosing frames, or decorative woodgrain unrelated to the subject.
```

## Preparation and checks

- Use two pigment pairs. Start with cutoff `0.065`.
- Keep the wide carved channels transparent, not white paint.
- Check that large masses remain separate at page size.
- If it resembles Stamped, enlarge a few ink masses and use broader cuts, not extra detail.
