# 08 · Screenprint

Flat spot-color layers with subtle mesh grain and small registration errors.
This is more evenly layered than Stamped and less carved than Carved.

Read the [shared prompt](../../references/prompts.md). Add the actual scene and palette, then this style block.

## Style prompt

```text
Make a small handmade screenprint interpretation of the reference.
Reduce the scene to a few flat shapes in two or three restrained spot inks.
Keep the main silhouette and the spaces between important forms recognizable.

Let separate color layers overlap in a few places.
Show a slight registration shift, subtle mesh grain, and small interruptions in ink coverage.
Use a small area of sparse halftone marks only where it helps describe shade.

Keep edges slightly imperfect rather than digitally precise.
Leave light areas and open spaces unprinted.
The image must remain an irregular arrangement of printed forms, not a rectangular poster.

Avoid smooth gradients, airbrush shading, dense mechanical halftones across the whole scene, and excessive distressed texture.
Do not add poster lettering, a print border, or a paper sheet.
```

## Preparation and checks

- Start with cutoff `0.065`. Map each main spot ink separately.
- Preserve small gaps and any intended registration offset.
- Keep mesh grain inside printed forms, not across the empty background.
- Check that halftones remain secondary and do not make this look like Stippled.
