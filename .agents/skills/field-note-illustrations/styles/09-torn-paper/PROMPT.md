# 09 · Torn paper

Large matte paper shapes with irregular, fibrous edges.
This uses layered shapes, not the many separate pieces of Tile mosaic.

Read the [shared prompt](../../references/prompts.md). Add the actual scene and palette, then this style block.

## Style prompt

```text
Rebuild the reference as an illustration made from a few pieces of torn, dyed paper.
Use two or three muted colors from the supplied palette.
Preserve the important silhouettes and relative positions while simplifying small details.

Make the pieces broad and deliberately uneven.
Show torn notches, thin fibrous edges, and small variations within the paper pigment.
Overlap a few pieces to describe foreground and background forms.

Keep large gaps between the important forms.
Let the empty canvas describe light areas, sky, and water when present.
Use larger pieces rather than many tiny fragments.

Keep the result flat and matte.
Do not add cast shadows, curled corners, a backing sheet, photographic craft materials, or a framed collage.
Avoid clean vector polygons and smooth scissor-cut edges on every piece.
```

## Preparation and checks

- Start with cutoff `0.065`. Retain torn edge fibers that belong to the shapes.
- Do not preserve a pale rectangle behind the pieces.
- Check that overlaps read through shape and color, not simulated shadows.
- Keep the large pieces distinct from Tile mosaic's small tesserae.
