# 10 · Wax crayon

Blunt wax strokes, grainy rubbings, and broken coverage.
The strokes stay firmer than Soft pastel's blended powder.

Read the [shared prompt](../../references/prompts.md). Add the actual scene and palette, then this style block.

## Style prompt

```text
Draw a loose observational version of the reference with wax crayons.
Use two or three muted pigments and a restrained earthy accent.
Keep the distinctive contours and essential scene relationships.

Use blunt, uneven lines and broad side-of-crayon rubbings.
Let the wax catch in small irregular marks, leaving many tiny gaps within each stroke.
Vary pressure and direction with a few overlapping passes.

Keep the drawing open and lightly worked.
Shade only selected forms. Leave large light areas empty.
Let some edges remain unfinished rather than outlining every object.

Avoid smooth fills, polished vector lines, wet paint blooms, heavy pastel blending, and shiny wax highlights.
The broken grain belongs inside the marks, not on a visible paper rectangle behind the scene.
```

## Preparation and checks

- Start with cutoff `0.065`. Keep grain gaps transparent.
- Map each used pigment separately to retain overlapping color strokes.
- Do not blur grain into smooth shading during preparation.
- At page size, the blunt strokes must differ from Pencil's fine lines and Soft pastel's soft masses.
