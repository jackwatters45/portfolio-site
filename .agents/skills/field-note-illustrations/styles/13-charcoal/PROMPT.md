# 13 · Charcoal

Powdery dark strokes, selective smudges, and lifted highlights.
This is heavier than Pencil and less colorful than Soft pastel.

Read the [shared prompt](../../references/prompts.md). Add the actual scene and palette, then this style block.

## Style prompt

```text
Draw the reference as a restrained charcoal or soft-graphite field study.
Use a dark neutral from the supplied palette, with one tiny earthy accent only when appropriate.
Keep the main silhouette and essential scene relationships clear.

Combine firm charcoal contours with broad side-of-stick strokes.
Use crumbly edges, localized finger-smudged tone, and a few sharp dark accents.
Lift pigment from selected light areas, leaving broken highlights and open gaps.

Keep the darkest tones in a few important forms.
Allow secondary details to remain unfinished.
Preserve large empty areas rather than shading the full scene.

Do not add charcoal dust across the whole canvas, a gray paper sheet, or a rectangular tonal background.
Avoid precise technical hatching and smooth airbrushed shading.
```

## Preparation and checks

- Use the theme's graphite or dark neutral as the primary target, not forest by default.
- Start with cutoff `0.065`. Preserve soft edges without keeping a field of dust behind the subject.
- Keep lifted highlights transparent.
- Check that smudges remain attached to meaningful forms and do not create a dirty background box.
