# 14 · Sgraffito

Fine scratches through dense oil-pastel shapes.
The scratches are narrower than Carved's gouge channels and less regular than Woodgrain's flowing lines.

Read the [shared prompt](../../references/prompts.md). Add the actual scene and palette, then this style block.

## Style prompt

```text
Interpret the reference as an oil-pastel sgraffito illustration.
Build a few dense, matte pigment shapes, then scratch narrow lines through them.
Use two restrained theme pigments.
An earthy pigment may carry the larger forms, with darker green for selected smaller forms.

Use uneven scratched channels, short incised marks, and a few wider scraped passages.
Let the scratches reveal empty space rather than white paint.
Follow the source's important contours and surface directions.

Keep the major forms separate, with large open light areas between them.
Leave the outer edges rough and slightly crumbly.
Preserve the essential scene relationships without adding dense decorative patterns.

Avoid a fully coated rectangular board, glossy pastel, thick carved channels, and regular crosshatching over every surface.
```

## Preparation and checks

- Start with cutoff `0.065`. Use separate pairs for the two main pigments.
- Do not force brown to remain a tiny accent if the chosen composition uses it as the main pigment.
- Keep scratched openings transparent and readable at page size.
- If fine scratches disappear, request fewer, slightly wider scratches rather than adding a white line overlay.
