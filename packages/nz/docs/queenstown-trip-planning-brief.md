# Queenstown Trip — Planning Brief

## Trip Overview

- **Destination:** Queenstown, New Zealand
- **Group size:** 7 people
- **Dates:** Sunday 11 October 2026 – Sunday 18 October 2026
- **Origin:** Melbourne, Australia
- **Arrival:** Jetstar JQ219 on Sunday 11 October. Melbourne Tullamarine T2 International 2:50 PM → Queenstown 8:00 PM.
- **Departure:** Jetstar JQ220 on Sunday 18 October. Queenstown 12:10 PM → Melbourne Tullamarine T2 International 1:35 PM.
- **Flight times:** Local to each airport. Flights are booked.
- **Local transport:** No car except for the Glenorchy road-trip day. Use walking, public buses, and activity shuttles otherwise.
- **Trip style:** Relaxed and flexible. The goal is not to create a tightly scheduled seven-day itinerary.
- **Season:** Mid-October / spring.
- **General approach:** A few memorable planned activities mixed with hikes, pubs, food, good weather, wandering around Queenstown, and deliberately unplanned time.

## Core Planning Philosophy

This trip should **not** feel over-programmed.

The group will be based primarily in Queenstown for the entire week. Most days should have enough flexibility to respond to weather, energy levels, work commitments, hangovers, and spontaneous ideas.

The rough target is:

- 2–3 larger / more expensive activities
- 2 good local hikes
- 1 relaxed wine outing
- Onsen / hot pools
- Plenty of time in Queenstown itself
- Several mornings, afternoons, or potentially entire days with little or nothing scheduled

Activities do **not** need to be assigned permanently to specific dates yet. Weather and reservation availability should determine the final schedule closer to the trip.

# Major Activities

## 1. Full-Day Road Trip — Glenorchy

This is currently the clearest major activity.

### Concept

Rent a vehicle large enough for all 7 people for one day and take a full-day road trip from Queenstown toward Glenorchy and beyond.

**Base route:**

Queenstown → Bob’s Cove / Picnic Point → Bennett’s Bluff → Glenorchy waterfront and lagoon → Queenstown.

Optional: replace the full lagoon walk with the Routeburn Nature Walk, subject to rental approval and road conditions. Paradise is an alternative extension, not an additional required stop.

### Purpose

The rental vehicle should be used for something that would be difficult or inconvenient to experience using normal local transportation. This is not simply a trip to another town; the drive, scenery, freedom to stop, and access to areas beyond Glenorchy are the point.

### Desired feel

- Full-day outing
- Scenic drive
- Lots of flexibility for stops
- Mountain / lake scenery
- Potential short walks
- Ideally one worthwhile walk or hike during the day
- No need to rush
- Return to Queenstown that evening

### Scheduling

Preferred days, roughly in order:

1. **Saturday 17 October** — ideal because there are no weekday work commitments.
2. **Monday 12 October** — best weekday option.
3. **Friday 16 October** — possible alternative.

The actual day should ultimately be chosen based on weather.

### Rental researched on 22 September 2026

GO Rentals offered an **eight-seat Kia Carnival Hybrid** for **Saturday 17 October, 8 AM–6 PM**, collected and returned at Queenstown Airport:

- **NZ$302 total with Basic cover:** NZ$7,000 excess and NZ$3,000 bond.
- **NZ$369 total with Peace of Mind:** NZ$1,500 excess and no bond. This vehicle does not have zero-excess cover.
- Totals include the NZ$279 rental, NZ$20 location fee and NZ$3 road-care fee. Fuel, optional extras and any payment fees are additional.
- The 6 PM return is outside the branch’s 8 AM–5 PM hours; arrange it in advance.
- Quote only, not held or booked. Other dates were not priced.

GO’s terms distinguish formed public roads from unformed/off-road tracks. Get written approval for the exact Routeburn or Paradise extension and vehicle before booking. Skippers Road, the Macetown road and water-crossing damage are excluded from cover.

Cancellation is not free: more than 24 hours ahead costs 10% of the rental plus refund fees; less than 24 hours costs 100%. Online cancellation requires at least 48 hours and adds 2% of the refund. Other refund administration fees are 10% of the refund or NZ$20, whichever is higher.

### Stops and walks

- **Bob’s Cove / Picnic Point:** 14 km from Queenstown. Allow 1–1.5 hours for bush, lake and viewpoint. Use the marked track; the old bridle track is closed.
- **Bennett’s Bluff:** about 25 km from Queenstown; 10–15 minutes at the signed lookout.
- **Glenorchy waterfront and lagoon:** red shed, jetty, lunch and a 1–2-hour wetland walk. Enter from Mull Street, not Glenorchy–Paradise Road. Skip flooded sections.
- **Optional Routeburn Nature Walk:** easy forest loop, 40 minutes–1 hour. The trailhead adds 35 minutes’ driving each way from Glenorchy, including narrow gravel road. Replace the full lagoon loop to keep the day relaxed.
- **Paradise alternative:** 20 km beyond Glenorchy; the last 12 km are gravel. Avoid fords and do not add both extensions by default.

Queenstown–Glenorchy is about 45 km / 45 minutes each way without stops. Leave Glenorchy around 4 PM for refuelling and the 6 PM return. Leave Routeburn Shelter about 35 minutes earlier if using that extension.

Live route-specific road conditions remain unverified. Check QLDC road updates and DOC track alerts before departure; September research cannot establish October conditions. An old 2020 Routeburn Road closure was not treated as a current alert.

Full quote, contract clauses, timing and sources: [Glenorchy road-trip research](research/glenorchy-road-trip.md).

## 2. Adventure Day

Reserve one day or substantial portion of a day for something exciting / adventurous.

The group has mixed levels of tolerance for extreme activities, so this should initially remain a **menu of possibilities rather than one fixed group activity**.

**Current front runner: [Skyline Gondola & Luge](https://queenstown.skyline.co.nz/things-to-do/queenstown-luge/).** Take the gondola up, then ride gravity-powered carts on dedicated downhill tracks above town. Use the chairlift for repeat rides and the gondola to return to town. This is a preference, not a booking or fixed group commitment. Published adult packages checked on 22 September 2026: gondola + 3 / 5 / 6 luge rides cost NZ$99 / NZ$104 / NZ$106. Six rides for seven adults total NZ$742. Unlimited rides cost NZ$140 each, but are unavailable during peak holiday periods. These are current published prices, not confirmed October quotes.

Allow roughly 2–3 hours for six rides and gondola access. Arrive at the gondola base at least 45 minutes before the booked luge session. Complete the first ride within the 30-minute session and use remaining rides that day. Direct booking cancellations require at least 48 hours’ notice for a refund; partial-use refunds have separate limits.

Keep the front-runner preference in planning notes only, not on the public page. Full sources and restrictions: [Skyline Luge research](research/skyline-luge.md).

Other options researched on 22 September 2026. Published adult prices are NZD:

- Shotover Jet: NZ$199; allow 1 hour 45 minutes with the free town shuttle.
- Ziptrek Moa / Kea: NZ$238 / NZ$288 including the separate NZ$69 gondola ticket and October tour rates.
- Kawarau Bridge Bungy: from NZ$320; 43 m jump, free spectators, bus and photos/video included.
- Nevis Bungy: from NZ$395; 134 m jump, bus and photos/video included. Spectators cost NZ$50.
- NZONE Skydive: from NZ$379 / NZ$469 / NZ$575 for 9,000 / 12,000 / 15,000 ft. Photos/video start at NZ$220 extra.

For skydiving, aim early in the trip and keep a backup day for weather.

Sources, transport, timings, restrictions and cancellation terms: [Onsen and adventure research](research/onsen-and-adventures.md).

Skydiving has specifically been floated by members of the group, although not everyone is interested.

It is completely acceptable for the group to split for this activity. There is no requirement that all seven people choose the same level of intensity.

Leave room for an adventure activity without making it a fixed group commitment.

# Hiking

The group wants approximately **two hikes during the week**.

### Requirements

- Accessible from Queenstown on foot or by public bus; no rental car
- Approximately 1–4 hours
- Not intended to be huge all-day missions
- Quality matters more than exact duration
- Scenic payoff preferred
- Easy to slot around work, weather, and other activities

Options researched on 22 September 2026. Times start at the track entrance and exclude travel there:

- **Queenstown Hill:** 2–3 hours return, moderate, about 4 km. Walk from town to Belfast Terrace.
- **Sawpit Gully:** 2–3 hours, 7.9 km loop, moderate with creek crossings. Take bus 4 to Arrowtown.
- **Sunshine Bay:** about 2 hours return, 4 km, easy–moderate. Add the 20-minute walk from town in each direction.
- **Tiki Trail / Skyline:** allow 2–3 hours return, 4.2 km, steep with about 480 m climbing. Walk to the gondola base.

Suggested pair: Queenstown Hill and Sawpit Gully. Sunshine Bay is the gentler alternative. Keep both dates flexible for weather.

Route links, access and source notes: [Car-free hikes and wine](research/car-free-hikes-and-wine.md).

- Pubs
