# Car-free hikes and wine

Researched: 22 September 2026. Trip: 11–18 October 2026.

## Planning constraint

The group will have a rental car only for the Glenorchy road-trip day. Use walking, public buses, or operator transport otherwise.

No activity dates or reservations were made during this research. Prices are published adult NZD prices, not held quotes.

## Hike options

Walking times below start at the track entrance. They exclude walking or bus travel to that entrance.

| Hike | Route and time | Difficulty | Car-free access |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Queenstown Hill | About 4 km return; 2–3 hours | Moderate | Walk from central Queenstown via Ballarat Street and Kerry Drive to Belfast Terrace |
| Sawpit Gully, Arrowtown | 7.9 km loop; 2–3 hours | Moderate, with steep sections and creek crossings | Orbus 4 from central Queenstown to Arrowtown; walk to the Chinese Settlement area |
| Sunshine Bay | 4 km return; about 2 hours | Easy–moderate, with undulating ground | Start near the Fernhill roundabout, about 20 minutes on foot from central Queenstown |
| Tiki Trail / Skyline | 2.1 km each way; allow 2–3 hours return with stops | Steep; about 480 m climbing | Walk to Cemetery Road, off Brecon Street, beside the gondola base |

### Suggested pair

Queenstown Hill and Sawpit Gully offer different scenery. Sunshine Bay is the gentler alternative. Tiki Trail suits a steeper outing.

These remain options, not a fixed schedule. Sawpit Gully needs additional time for both bus journeys and any Arrowtown lunch stop.

### Queenstown Hill

The route climbs through pine forest to the Basket of Dreams lookout. The summit offers a wider view over the Whakatipu basin.

Destination Queenstown's dedicated article gives two hours return, plus 30 minutes for the summit. Its broader guide gives 2–3 hours and 2 km each way. The site uses the broader allowance and an approximate distance because the summit extension changes the route length.

- https://www.queenstownnz.co.nz/stories/post/queenstown-hill-time-walk/
- https://www.queenstownnz.co.nz/stories/post/10-beautiful-walks-in-queenstown/

### Sawpit Gully

The loop follows the Arrow River, crosses the gully, and returns along Bush Creek. It includes mining remains and views towards Lake Hayes and the Remarkables. Choose dry conditions where possible because of the creek crossings and steep sections.

**Transport correction:** older search results describe route 2 as a direct Queenstown–Arrowtown service. The current network uses route 4 through central Queenstown. Route 2 now links Frankton Hub and Arrowtown. The page links the current route 4 timetable.

### Sunshine Bay

The track follows native bush beside Lake Whakatipu to the beach and jetty. Add the 20-minute approach from town in each direction. It is not a flat paved promenade.

The official guide lists 4 km return and two hours. Shorter timings on other sites use different starting points or a faster pace. The public page uses the official guide's allowance.

- https://www.queenstownnz.co.nz/stories/post/10-beautiful-walks-in-queenstown/

### Tiki Trail

The official guides give 2.1 km and about one hour uphill. Skyline gives an elevation gain of 480 m. The public page allows 2–3 hours for the return outing, including stops.

Walk down to keep it free. Skyline says one-way gondola tickets are available at the top souvenir shop. No one-way fare is quoted because it was not verified.

- https://www.queenstownnz.co.nz/listing/tiki-trail/9099/
- https://www.queenstownnz.co.nz/stories/post/10-beautiful-walks-in-queenstown/
- https://www.skyline.co.nz/blog/tiki-trail-queenstown/

### Options not included

- Ben Lomond summit: DOC gives 14 km and 6–8 hours return from the Tiki Trail. It exceeds the brief.
- Ben Lomond saddle: DOC gives five hours return from town, or 3–4 hours from the gondola top. Higher sections can have spring snow and ice.
- Frankton Arm: Destination Queenstown reports a track detour throughout 2026 for infrastructure work. Sunshine Bay provides a simpler lakeside choice.
- Bob's Cove and Moke Lake: keep these for road-trip research rather than assuming another rental day.

## Wine transport

### Hop On Hop Off — afternoon pass

- NZ$99.50 per adult. Tastings and food are additional.
- The Sunday–Friday timetable departs the Station Building, Camp Street, at 12:10 PM.
- The last return to the Station Building is 5:30 PM.
- The timetable runs in one direction. A bus does not return to a stop it has already passed on that run.
- Venue bookings must fit the timetable. A bus booking does not reserve a tasting or lunch.
- Saturday has a separate timetable; the published example below uses Sunday–Friday services.

### Wine Hopper Bus — flexible shuttle

- From NZ$99 per adult. Tastings and food are additional.
- Daily Queenstown departure at noon, outside Red Rock Cafe, 48 Camp Street.
- Also lists Frankton and Arrowtown pickups.
- The driver arranges pickup times between wineries instead of using a fixed loop.
- Agree the return time with the driver on the day.
- The operator says most guests visit 3–4 venues. Two tastings and lunch better match this trip's slower pace.

Sources:
- https://www.winehopperbus.co.nz/
- https://www.winehopperbus.co.nz/contact--bookings.html

The Wine Hopper FAQ still suggests typical tasting fees of NZ$10–20. Current venue pages show higher prices. Use the venue prices below instead.

## Winery shortlist

### Gibbston Valley — Classic Cave

- From NZ$30 per adult.
- 30 minutes, three wine tastings, the cave, and historic vines.
- Current Classic Cave page lists tours at 10 AM, 11 AM, 1 PM and 3 PM.
- A separate transport-inclusive option exists. Do not add it when buying a winery bus pass.
- The website's country routes redirect based on location. The public tour link resolves to the Classic Cave page.

Sources:
- https://www.gibbstonvalley.com/experiences/tours/cave-tour/
- https://gibbstonvalley.com/au/experiences/tours/classic-cave/

### Kinross — Classic Experience

- NZ$30 per adult; 45 minutes and five local wines.
- Kinross represents Hawkshead, Valli, High Garden, Wild Irishman and its own wines.
- Cellar door open daily, 10 AM–5 PM; last tasting at 4:30 PM.
- Bistro lunch is served 11 AM–3 PM.
- Food is not served during the Classic tasting. Book lunch separately before or after it.
- Kinross does not operate its own Queenstown shuttle.

Sources:
- https://kinross.nz/pages/wine-tasting-experiences
- https://kinross.nz/pages/faqs

The live rendered page showed NZ$30 for Classic, distinct from the NZ$25 Kinross-only Wine Experience.

### Brennan Wines

- Self-guided flights from NZ$45, with three wines of your choice.
- Hosted tasting: NZ$65, five wines and appetisers; allow one hour.
- The vineyard offers Pinot Noir and less common local varieties, including Muscat, Tempranillo and Zinfandel.
- Current venue page: open daily, 11 AM–5 PM. Reservations are strongly encouraged.
- Address: 86 Gibbston Back Road.

Source: https://www.brennanwines.com/Visit

The current rendered venue page was used. An older cellar-door URL returned 404, and the bus operator's stop list has different opening days.

## Suggested wine afternoon

Gibbston Valley's cave tour plus Kinross lunch and Classic tasting is a possible pair, not a reservation.

The published Sunday–Friday bus timetable makes this sequence possible if venue bookings are available:

1. Depart central Queenstown at 12:10 PM; arrive at Gibbston Valley at 12:50 PM.
2. Request the 1 PM Classic Cave tour, which lasts 30 minutes.
3. Catch the 1:40 PM bus; arrive at Kinross at 1:52 PM.
4. Book lunch before the 3 PM lunch cutoff and a separate afternoon tasting.
5. Leave Kinross no later than the last pickup at 4:48 PM; return to town around 5:30 PM.

Bookable capacity for the October trip dates was not checked. Confirm the bus and venue times together before paying.

Estimated base cost: NZ$99.50 + NZ$30 + NZ$30 = NZ$159.50 per adult, before lunch, bottles, and any applicable card fees. The page rounds this to about NZ$160. Brennan is an alternative rather than a required third stop.
