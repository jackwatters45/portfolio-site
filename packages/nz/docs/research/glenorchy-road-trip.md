# Glenorchy road trip and one-day vehicle hire

Checked **22 September 2026**. Prices are **NZD**. No vehicle was reserved, no enquiry was sent, and no payment or driver details were entered.

## Recommendation

Use an eight-seat people mover for one day. Prefer **Saturday 17 October**, with Monday 12 or Friday 16 as weather alternatives. Only Saturday was priced.

Make Queenstown → Bob’s Cove → Bennett’s Bluff → Glenorchy → Queenstown the base route. The Queenstown–Glenorchy road is sealed. Add the Routeburn Nature Walk only if the rental company approves the road and conditions allow it. Replace the full lagoon walk rather than trying to fit every walk into one day.

Paradise is an alternative scenic extension, not a required stop. Do not combine both extensions by default. Beyond Glenorchy, a public gravel road is not the same as an off-road track. Rental permission, vehicle suitability and actual road conditions are separate checks.

## Rental road restrictions

GO’s contract, clause 16.1.10, excludes cover on Skippers Road, the road to Macetown and other named prohibited roads. It also excludes unformed roads and tracks, including gravel routes that are not officially designated roads.

This is **not a blanket ban on all public gravel roads**, but it is also **not written approval for a particular extension**. Before booking, ask GO to confirm the selected vehicle may use:

1. Glenorchy–Routeburn Road to Routeburn Shelter.
2. Glenorchy–Paradise Road, specifying the intended endpoint if Paradise is chosen.

Clause 16.1.3 excludes off-road/bogged-vehicle recovery. Clause 16.1.15 excludes water/submersion damage, including creek, river and flooded-ford crossings. Clause 16.1.16 excludes damage from incorrectly fitted snow chains. An insurance upgrade does not remove these exclusions.

For this trip: use designated roads and car parks, obey closures, do not cross flowing water or flooded fords, and turn back if the road is unsafe. Do not add Skippers Canyon, Macetown or an off-road shortcut.

## Rental cancellation

GO’s published clauses 23.3–23.4 state:

- Cancel **more than 24 hours** before pickup: 10% of the total rental cost is retained.
- Cancel **less than 24 hours** before pickup, or fail to appear: 100% is retained.
- Online cancellation must occur at least **48 hours** before pickup.
- Online cancellation refunds have a further **2% refund administration fee**.
- Other refunds have an administration fee of **10% of the refund due or NZ$20, whichever is higher**.
- The wording does not clearly define exactly 24 hours; do not leave cancellation to that boundary.

This is not a fully refundable weather-backup booking. Read the final booking terms and discuss date changes before committing. Retain the chosen return time even if dropping the vehicle early; an early return does not automatically earn a refund.

## Stops and walks

Times below are walk/stop allowances, not the full trip from Queenstown. All three DOC walks are free day walks.

### 1. Bob’s Cove and Picnic Point

- The DOC car park is **14 km from Queenstown**, on the Glenorchy road.
- DOC lists **1.5 km one way**, 20 minutes to the cove and 30 minutes to Picnic Point. Allow **1–1.5 hours** for the return walk and photo stops.
- Native bush, lake access, a historic lime kiln and a short, steep climb to the viewpoint.
- Use the marked track. DOC says the nearby **old bridle track is permanently closed**.
- Use the designated car park. Do not park on the roadside or reverse into the main road. Skip the stop if safe parking is unavailable.

https://www.doc.govt.nz/parks-and-recreation/places-to-go/otago/places/queenstown-area/things-to-do/bobs-cove-track-and-nature-walk/

### 2. Bennett’s Bluff

- About **25 km from Queenstown** on the same drive.
- Allow **10–15 minutes** for the lake and mountain viewpoint.
- Use the signed lookout/car park rather than stopping on a blind bend.

https://www.queenstownnz.co.nz/stories/post/scenic-drives-from-queenstown-explore-more-at-your-own-pace/

### 3. Glenorchy waterfront and lagoon

- Queenstown–Glenorchy is approximately **45 km / 45 minutes each way without stops**. Allow more time for bends, traffic and the rental depot.
- Stop at the red shed and jetty, then use the signed **Mull Street** access to the Glenorchy Walkway.
- DOC gives **1–2 hours**, easy–intermediate, with wetland boardwalks, birds and mountain views.
- Do **not** park or enter from Glenorchy–Paradise Road; DOC identifies it as an accident hotspot.
- The lagoon section floods after heavy or prolonged rain. Skip it if flooded.
- Have lunch in Glenorchy or bring a picnic. Do not depend on finding food beyond town.

https://www.doc.govt.nz/parks-and-recreation/places-to-go/otago/places/glenorchy-area/things-to-do/glenorchy-walkway/

### Optional: Routeburn Nature Walk

- DOC rates this an **easy short walk, 40 minutes–1 hour**.
- Start at **Routeburn Shelter**, cross the first swingbridge and follow the main track for 10–15 minutes. Take the signed nature-walk turnoff. The forest loop rejoins the main track at Sugarloaf Stream; return to the shelter.
- This is a low-level forest walk, **not** the Routeburn Great Walk or an alpine day hike.
- The road end is about **35 minutes from Glenorchy each way**. The final section is gravel and narrow through forest. DOC warns it may not suit large campervans.
- Allow roughly **2–2.5 extra hours** for the drive, walk and stops. Substitute it for the full lagoon walk to keep the day relaxed.
- Get rental permission and check road/track notices first. The nine/ten-seat alternatives are not automatically suitable merely because they have enough seats.

https://www.doc.govt.nz/parks-and-recreation/places-to-go/otago/places/mount-aspiring-national-park/things-to-do/tracks/routeburn-nature-walk/

### Paradise alternative

Access Glenorchy describes Paradise as **20 km beyond Glenorchy: 8 km sealed, then 12 km unsealed**. The road passes farmland, Diamond Lake and beech forest. There is no cafe or shop. Paradise Trust is private land and charges for access to its grounds.

Do not treat the full Paradise/Chinamans Bluff drive as a standard sealed-road outing. Confirm the exact endpoint with GO, check conditions and avoid fords or water crossings. Routeburn is the clearer choice if a forest walk is the main aim.

https://accessglenorchy.co.nz/paradise/

## Flexible day outline

This is a planning allowance, not a booked schedule:

- **8 AM:** collect the vehicle; allow time to gather everyone and leave Queenstown.
- **Morning:** Bob’s Cove, then Bennett’s Bluff.
- **Late morning / lunch:** Glenorchy waterfront, lagoon walk and lunch.
- **Alternative afternoon:** replace the full lagoon loop with the Routeburn Nature Walk if approved and conditions are good.
- **By about 4 PM:** leave Glenorchy for Queenstown. Leave Routeburn Shelter roughly 35 minutes earlier if using that extension.
- Refuel and return the vehicle **by 6 PM**, following the agreed after-hours process.

Do not try to add Paradise as another compulsory stop. In poor conditions, use the shorter sealed-road outing or move the road trip to another day, subject to rental change/cancellation terms.

## Road conditions and limits of this check

- The three DOC walk pages were checked in the live browser. The Bob’s Cove bridle-track closure and lagoon flooding warning are current published notices. No promise is made that a road or track will be open on the trip date.
- QLDC’s current winter-road page was read in the browser. It directs users to daily morning reports, community alerts and QLDC Facebook for updates during the day. It also warns that conditions can change quickly.
- **A reliable live, route-specific road status was not established.** The general QLDC page is not an all-clear for Glenorchy, Routeburn or Paradise, and September conditions cannot establish October conditions.
- An apparently recent search hit about a Routeburn Road/Scott Creek washout led to a **4 February 2020** QLDC notice. It was not treated as a current closure.
- Before departure, check QLDC updates for local roads and the linked DOC pages for track alerts. Ask locally if the update does not cover the intended extension. Follow closure signs on the road.
- If snow, ice, flooding or chain requirements affect the route, change the plan unless the vehicle, equipment and driver are suitable. GO lists snow chains as a paid extra; do not assume they are included.
