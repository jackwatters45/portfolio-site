# Onsen and adventure options

Researched: 22 September 2026. Trip: 11–18 October 2026.

Prices are NZD per adult unless stated otherwise. These are published prices, not held quotes. No bookings were made.

## Summary

- Original Onsen suits the group with two simultaneous pools, split 4 + 3. Total: NZ$485, or about NZ$69.29 each when split evenly.
- Shotover Jet offers a short canyon ride without a jump from height.
- Ziptrek Moa offers four ziplines. Kea adds two lines, more time, and a steeper final descent.
- Kawarau has free spectators and a shorter outing than Nevis. Nevis is the larger jump.
- NZONE recommends skydiving early in the trip, with a backup day for weather. Photos add a substantial cost.

## Original Onsen — soak only

### Price and capacity

- Maximum four bathers per Original pool.
- Three bathers: NZ$225 per pool. Four bathers: NZ$260 per pool.
- Two pools: NZ$485 total. Equal split: NZ$485 ÷ 7 = NZ$69.29 each, rounded.
- The 60-minute room booking includes changing time. Allow 1–2 hours for the visit.
- Towels and drinking water are included. Bring swimwear. Food and other drinks cost extra.
- The pools overlook the Shotover River and have retractable roofs.

Primary source: https://www.onsen.co.nz/experiences/original-onsen-soak-only/

### Transport and terms

- Free shuttle from the bus stop outside Marmolada Cafe, 43 Camp Street, Queenstown. Request it when booking.
- No accommodation pickup or drop-off.
- The return drive takes about 15 minutes. Use the pickup time supplied by the operator.
- If arriving independently, arrive 15 minutes before a soak-only session.
- Changes or cancellations need at least 24 hours' notice. Less notice costs the full booking price.
- The longer 72-hour notice rule applies to bookings of three or more pools, not this two-pool plan.

Primary source: https://www.onsen.co.nz/about/faqs/

### Shotover Jet

- NZ$199 adult rate is published as valid for travel through 30 September 2027.
- Free shuttle departs from the Station Building, 25 Shotover Street, on selected departures.
- Shuttle check-in is 45 minutes before the ride. Self-drive check-in is 30 minutes before the ride.
- Self-drive address: 3 Arthurs Point Road.
- Customer cancellations within 24 hours are non-refundable.
- If the operator cancels for weather or river conditions, it offers a refund or reschedule. Third-party bookings use that seller's terms.
- The current price page says photo/video purchases are temporarily unavailable during an upgrade. They are not included in the comparison.
- The operator asks guests with relevant injuries, medical conditions, or access needs to contact it before booking.

### Ziptrek Moa and Kea

- Use the **1 October 2026 onward** rate, not the lower September rate: Moa NZ$169; Kea NZ$219.
- These rates cover 1 October–18 December 2026, including this trip.
- The current Skyline adult gondola price is NZ$69. The totals above combine this with the October zipline prices.
- The gondola fare is a published current price, not a date-specific held quote.
- Allow 40–60 minutes to access the upper Skyline complex. Allow longer during busy periods.
- Check in at the Ziptrek Treehouse 15 minutes before departure.
- Moa starts and ends at the top. There is a short uphill walk after the last line.
- Kea includes a forest walk and descends the mountain. A gondola ticket can be stamped for re-entry afterward.
- Maximum weight: 125 kg. Kea minimum: 30 kg. Closed-toe shoes are required.
- Guests must manage stairs and uneven trails. The operator excludes pregnant participants.
- Bookings below ten people can cancel free up to 24 hours before departure. Later cancellation or a no-show costs the full fare.
- Tours run in rain and snow within operating limits. Rain alone does not mean cancellation.

### Kawarau and Nevis Bungy

- Current advertised starting prices: Kawarau NZ$320; Nevis NZ$395.
- Both include photos, video, the activity tee and Queenstown bus transport on the product pages.
- Kawarau: 43 m. Spectators and the spectator bus are free; reserve bus space.
- Nevis: 134 m. Spectators cost NZ$50.
- Kawarau weight range: 35–150 kg; the maximum may fall to 130 kg at high river levels.
- Nevis weight range: 45–127 kg.
- Refunds for customer cancellations require **more than 48 hours' notice**. The 1.99% card transaction fee is not refunded.
- Changes inside 48 hours incur a NZ$50 administration fee. No-shows and choosing not to jump are generally non-refundable.
- Operator cancellation for unsafe weather offers a refund or reschedule.
- Read the operator's health restrictions before choosing a jump.

### NZONE Skydive

- Current published starting prices: 9,000 ft NZ$379; 12,000 ft NZ$469; 15,000 ft NZ$575.
- These were read from the live rendered price page, not an old search snippet. October seat availability was not checked.
- Free return transport from 35 Shotover Street, Queenstown.
- Typical total time is 3.5 hours. The terms say to keep the day free because weather and air traffic can delay the jump.
- The operator recommends an early-trip morning and a backup day. Do not leave skydiving until departure day.
- Optional handicam photos/video: NZ$220. Dedicated freefall photographer: NZ$333, available on the day. Combined package: NZ$395.
- A 15,000 ft jump with handicam photos/video starts at NZ$795, before applicable card fees.
- Customers at 95 kg or above need a Tandem Master assessment. Minimum weight is 40 kg. Final participation depends on harness fit and operator assessment.
- No alcohol or recreational drugs within eight hours of the jump. Read the operator's medical and activity restrictions.
- Customer cancellations up to 24 hours before departure incur no fee. Later cancellation costs the full fare.
- Weather cancellation: reschedule; if that is not possible, request a refund.
