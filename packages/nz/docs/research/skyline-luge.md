## Experience and timing

- Gondola up to the complex, gravity-powered carts on dedicated downhill tracks, and a chairlift for repeat rides. Return to town by gondola, not by luge.
- Skyline lists an average of 15 minutes per luge ride. Allow roughly 2–3 hours for six rides, gondola access and queues. This is a planning estimate, not an operator-guaranteed duration.
- Select a luge session when booking online. Arrive at the base gondola terminal at least 45 minutes before that session.
- Complete the first ride within the allocated 30-minute session. Remaining rides can be taken later that day during operating hours.
- All rides on a ticket must be used the same day.
- For an operational delay, including a temporary weather closure, affected tickets move to the next available session.
- Confirm opening hours and session availability for the chosen October date before booking.

## Cancellation and safety

- Direct bookings require at least 48 hours’ notice for cancellation or amendment refunds. No refund for a no-show or changes within 48 hours. Third-party bookings have separate terms.
- Once the gondola has been used, the package is partially redeemed. Any eligible refund uses the difference from the closest lower-value package, not a per-ride calculation.
- The partial-use policy excludes change of mind, guest choice and weather that does not cause a full attraction closure. Operational interruptions are assessed under the policy; do not assume an automatic full refund.
- Helmets and footwear are required. Instructors provide a briefing before the first ride.
- Do not ride while impaired by alcohol or drugs. Group racing, bumping and skidding are prohibited.
- Pregnancy is not permitted. Skyline also lists heart conditions, motion sickness, back problems and other physical limitations in its health restrictions.
