## Around The Basin — Bike The Wineries Half Day

- Self-guided riding from The Church Cellar Door in Gibbston. This is not the longer Arrowtown ride.
- Regular mountain bikes and e-bikes offered. Includes Queenstown transfers, helmet, winery map, and trail briefing.
- Operator product data advertises **from NZ$105**, explicitly in NZD. No date-specific quote or verified e-bike upgrade price. Do not use the older Tourism New Zealand NZ$95–145 range as a current quote.
- Published September–April timetable: Queenstown shop check-in 11:50 AM, shuttle noon, ready to ride around 12:50 PM. Pickup at The Church at 4:30 PM, then return shuttle. Allow about five hours overall; this is a planning allowance, not a guaranteed return time.
- The product describes a 25-minute outbound shuttle. Confirm the Queenstown meeting point when booking; no accommodation pickup is established.
- The operator describes roughly 9 km of wine region to explore. Actual riding distance depends on the stops and return route. The trail trust describes the Gibbston River Wine Trail as easy / Grade 2. Riders still need confidence on gravel and varied terrain.
- Final pickup can be from another winery on the operator’s map. Call at least 45 minutes before departure to change it. Do not plan to cycle back to Queenstown or continue riding after dark.
- Tastings and food are excluded. Book wineries separately, especially for groups of six or more. Seven bikes, suitable sizes, e-bike prices, shuttle seats, and availability for 12–17 October remain unverified.
- Gibbston Valley and Kinross remain possible stops. The operator lists both on its winery guide. Confirm the riding sequence, cave-tour time, and Kinross lunch before committing. Brennan remains an alternative. Do not treat the whole shortlist as required.
- Use spittoons while cycling and save drinking for the final pickup stop. Never ride impaired. Helmets are required. Carry water and warm, waterproof layers; check spring conditions with the operator.

## Budget treatment

Use a single wine activity with mutually exclusive transport options:

- Bike package starting rate NZ$105 + existing Gibbston NZ$30 + Kinross NZ$30 = **from NZ$165 per adult / NZ$1,155 for seven**.
- Existing Hop On Hop Off NZ$99.50 + the same tastings = **NZ$159.50 per adult / NZ$1,116.50 for seven**.
- Bike starting estimate adds NZ$5.50 per adult / NZ$38.50 for seven. This does not price an e-bike upgrade.
- Lunch, bottles, and e-bike upgrades are excluded. These are not October quotes. The existing A$1,000 activity ceiling stays in AUD; no exchange rate is assumed.

## Cancellation caution

Direct single-day terms place seven riders in the 6–14 group: 10% non-refundable deposit, balance due seven days ahead, and full charges inside seven days for changes or cancellation.

The product and weather clauses mention a 3% refund fee. Other clauses specify 10% and retention of non-refundable deposits. Obtain written terms for this seven-person booking. Do not promise a free last-minute switch to the bus, or reserve both transport options as required activities.
