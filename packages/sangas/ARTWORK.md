Use the approved Taco’s Land `close-up-painted.webp` and `lakeside-ink-wash-detail-light.webp` as direct style references. Style names alone did not match closely enough. The first blue paintings had the right technique but the wrong palette. Preserve broad painted shapes and light ink washes rather than detailed food-sketch outlines.

Use natural bread tan `#cfbea0`, toasted brown `#49372a`, olive green `#526b3e`, and muted red `#b96550`. Do not colour the food blue. Only the cover includes the table, paper and floor detail. Food paintings include complete trays without surrounding backgrounds.

Final assets are transparent, lossless WebP. Painted images use palette-based matting with cutoff `0.065` and 20-pixel padding. The cover received a targeted repair to complete its lower sandwich and tray. It retains native transparency, with alpha values at or below 2/255 removed before cropping and 20-pixel padding.

Exact prompts, source files, models, image URLs, dimensions and Paper nodes are recorded in `artwork/grazia.json`. Historical entries document rejected variants; only the three selected assets are shipped.
