The new photograph controls the subject; the approved artwork controls the brushwork. Only the cover includes surroundings. Food paintings include complete baskets without a table or other background.

The palette is brick red `#963d2e`, dark brown `#352b25`, and tan `#d9c6aa`. Final assets are transparent, lossless WebP. Preparation used palette-based matting with cutoff `0.065` and 20-pixel padding.

Exact prompts, source files, models, image URLs, dimensions and Paper nodes are recorded in `artwork/tacos-jaguar.json`. Historical entries document rejected variants; only the three selected assets are shipped.
