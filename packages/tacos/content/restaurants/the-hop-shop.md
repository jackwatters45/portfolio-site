What a weekend. Broke out the tandem bike, watched/played a shocking amount of footy, made 2 trips to the Erica, finally made it to karaoke, and caught a 6/10 sunset on the beach in beautiful Frankston. The food was terrible, but the company was not, with guest judges Heather and Katelyn joining us.

Service wasn’t great: messed-up drinks, a weirdly strict ID policy, and gross water. The food and drinks didn’t make up for it. They were out of a bunch of ingredients, Heather’s drink was undrinkable, and the food that arrived was average at best.

Meg has lost restaurant-picking privileges for the next two outings. We’ll leave her with sunset spot recs. Also, karaoke stage presence needs some more practice.
